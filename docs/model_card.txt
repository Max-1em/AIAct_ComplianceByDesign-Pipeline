Model Card

Model Name: Applicant Scoring Model
Model Type: Random Forest Classifier

Purpose:
This model is used to support HR in screening job applications.

Input Data:
- CV
- Education
- Work experience

Output:
Applicant score between 0 and 1 indicating suitability.

Training Data:
Synthetic HR dataset

Risks:
Potential bias in hiring decisions.

Human Oversight:
Final hiring decision is always made by a human recruiter.
